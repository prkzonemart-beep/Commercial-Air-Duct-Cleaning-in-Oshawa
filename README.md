Failed health and safety inspections. For restaurants, clinics, and schools in Oshawa, indoor air quality isn't optional. Contaminated ducts can put you at risk during inspections.
Unpleasant odours. Musty or stale smells in a commercial space drive customers away. Dirty ducts are one of the most common causes — and most business owners never make the connection.

What Professional Commercial Duct Cleaning Actually Involves
A proper commercial duct cleaning isn't a quick vacuum and done. A certified team will:

Inspect your entire duct network before touching anything
Use industrial-grade HEPA-filtered vacuum equipment to extract debris
Agitate buildup inside the ducts using rotary brush systems
Clean all supply and return vents, grilles, and registers
Service the air handler, blower motor, and coil if accessible
Provide a full before-and-after report

How Often Should Commercial Ducts Be Cleaned in Oshawa?
For most commercial buildings in Oshawa, NADCA recommends professional duct cleaning every 1 to 3 years, depending on:

The type of business (restaurants and clinics need more frequent cleaning)
Building size and number of occupants
Whether the space allows smoking or handles food
Recent renovations or construction work nearby

If your building has never been cleaned, start now — then build a regular schedule from there.

Signs Your Oshawa Commercial Building Needs Duct Cleaning Now
Don't wait until there's a visible problem. Book an inspection if you notice any of these:

Dust accumulating quickly on desks, shelves, and surfaces
Employees complaining of dry eyes, headaches, or allergies at work
Musty or stale odours coming from vents
Uneven temperature across different floors or zones
Noticeably higher energy costs with no other explanation
Visible mold, debris, or dark residue around vent grilles

Any one of these is enough reason to call. Two or more is urgent.
